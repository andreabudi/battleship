          How to play

          Goal

          Sink all 5 enemy ships before the AI sinks yours.

          Placing your fleet

          • Select a ship in the fleet list, then click a cell on Your waters to place it. On a phone, tap a cell to preview and press Confirm.
          • Press R or the Rotate (R) button to switch between horizontal and vertical.
          • Ships cannot overlap or go off the board; an invalid spot shows in red.
          • Click a placed ship to pick it up and move it. Use Undo last ship, Random placement or Clear all to change your mind.
          • Start game unlocks once all 5 ships are placed.

          Fleet: Carrier 5 · Battleship 4 · Cruiser 3 · Submarine 3 · Destroyer 2

          Playing

          • You and the AI take turns firing one shot each. Click a cell on Enemy waters to fire.
          • × Hit: a red cross on the cell. Miss: a dark cell with a dot. × Sunk: the whole ship darkens and its wreck is revealed.
          • The AI's shots appear on Your waters. The Battle log records every shot.

          Winning

          The first to sink the whole enemy fleet wins. New game starts over at any time.
